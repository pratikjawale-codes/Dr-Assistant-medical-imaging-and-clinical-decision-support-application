Medical AI Credibility & Evaluation Standards (Section 16 Plan):

• Never report raw accuracy without specifying class imbalance and patient-level test cohort splits.
• Explicitly segregate model activation maps (Grad-CAM) from clinical proof.
• Prioritize actionable triage alerts (reducing turnaround time for tension pneumothorax or acute consolidation).
